“Explain the Doctrine of Basic Structure. What are its origins?”
🤖 Doctrine of Basic Structure

The Basic Structure Doctrine is a judicial principle that certain fundamental features of the Constitution of India cannot be altered or destroyed through amendments by the Parliament.

Key Highlights
  • Originates from Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)
  • Prevents Parliament from damaging the “Spirit of the Constitution”
  • Maintains Supremacy of the Constitution
